# **App Name**: Swapna Poorti

## Core Features:

- Hero Image Display: Showcase 'IMG_20260326_100835.png' as a prominent hero image at the top of the landing page, incorporating a smooth fade-out effect at its bottom edge to transition into the text content.
- Marathi Message Presentation: Render the complete Marathi message provided by the user, ensuring highly readable typography and thoughtful spacing optimized for mobile viewing.
- Sectional Micro-Animations: Implement subtle, elegant animations for each major text section to provide an engaging and emotionally resonant scrolling experience, without distracting from the message.
- Mobile-First Responsive Layout: Design and optimize the entire landing page specifically for mobile devices, guaranteeing a seamless and visually appealing experience across various screen sizes.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color (for headings and key text elements): A rich, contemplative violet, #442C70, evoking wisdom and profound emotion.
- Background color: A very dark, almost black hue with a subtle hint of violet, #1C181E, creating a 'black canvas' for the content to stand out respectfully.
- Accent color (for highlights and interactive elements): A vibrant blue-violet, #5538F2, providing a touch of modern vibrancy and celebratory 'sparkle'.
- Headlines and emphasis text: 'Alegreya' (serif), chosen for its elegant, intellectual, and contemporary feel, suitable for highlighting key emotional phrases.
- Body text: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif), providing excellent readability and a warm, modern sensibility for the narrative's longer sections on mobile screens.
- Minimal and abstract iconography if any, complementing the emotional narrative without visual clutter.
- Clean, vertical stacking of content for mobile, with ample padding and margins to enhance readability and visual breathing room, creating an intimate storytelling experience.
- Subtle fade-ins for text blocks as they enter the viewport and gentle scrolling animations for the background elements, enriching the emotional depth without being overt.
